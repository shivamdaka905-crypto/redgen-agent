The house

Built in 1904. Restored by hand, twice.

Four generations of the same family have kept this courtyard house. The rooms are small on purpose, the windows are original, and the plaster is still mixed the old way. What we added: hot water that never runs out, fast fibre and mattresses you will ask about.

Every stay includes breakfast, filtered water, and a walking map drawn by our own guide.
